Muuvie redesign: all mockups so far

Same idea as the Abbay redesign (AbbayiOS #166), adapted to a movie app. Stop painting opaque fills over everything. Let the poster pick the screen's colour. Put glass only on controls that float over content. Use one poster component everywhere. Each screen is drawn twice: iOS Liquid Glass and ANDROID Material 3 Expressive. Both use the same layout, with each platform's own chrome. Posters are placeholder gradients and the copy is the app's real pt-BR. Click the options you prefer, then reply in the terminal.

4 · What carries across every screen (the Abbay "G6" equivalent)

  • One MuuviePoster widget replaces the poster drawing on every screen: one 2:3 shape, corner radius by size (10 iOS / 14–16 Android), a 0.5 px inner stroke, an optional shadow, and a placeholder tile for missing art.
  • One glass helper file (for example muuvie_glass.dart) is the only place that branches on platform. iOS gets glass, Android gets M3 tonal surfaces, and screens never check Platform.isIOS themselves. The existing MuuvieButton and platform_helper already lean this way.
  • Poster tint: ColorScheme.fromImageProvider (built into Flutter) on movie detail; the user's accent colour on Perfil, shelves and maratonas.
  • Subtraction: remove the opaque surfaceContainerLow panels, the two copied _TabBarDelegates, the hard-coded black in the trailer button and on the news article, and the three competing card styles.
  • The glass itself, in Flutter: Flutter has no native Liquid Glass. There are two routes:
    • (a) BackdropFilter blur with a highlight stroke, as drawn here. It works everywhere but costs GPU and only looks like glass.
    • (b) A shader package such as liquid_glass_renderer, or native UIKit views for the tab bar via platform views. Closer to the real thing, but less mature.
    This is the main technical question for the design doc. Right now my leaning is (a), with the tab bar as the one candidate for (b).

Not drawn yet: Social feed, Programação, Notícias, the actions sheet, and the rating screen. They'd follow whichever directions you pick here.
